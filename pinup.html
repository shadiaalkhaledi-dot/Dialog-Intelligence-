The Pin-up Wall

The rules we now follow, each one earned from a real win or fail. Check here before starting anything new — chances are a rule already applies.

What this wall is for

The Studio Floor tells you what each project is — status, risk, what we need. This wall is different on purpose: it's the standing rules we follow because a real win or fail earned them, independent of any one project. A rule can come from building a tool, or from building this site itself; either way it applies the next time, wherever "next time" happens to be. A "pin-up" is the old studio ritual: tack the work up and take the critique out loud, together. BIG ran it firm-wide every two weeks; this is the two-person prototype, and it scales the day the firm wants it to. The rule that keeps it honest, measured by the meter below: if a month passes with no FAIL pinned, we've either stopped experimenting or started hiding the misses.
