The Pin-up Wall

Dated pins, WIN or FAIL, equal typography. This wall is the actual feedback loop — the rest of the building is context.

What this wall is for

This is the studio's track record. The desks tell you what each tool is; this wall tells you whether we're still experimenting and staying honest about it — every attempt, win or fail, dated and given equal weight, newest on top. A "pin-up" is the old studio ritual: tack the work up and take the critique out loud, together. BIG ran it firm-wide every two weeks; this is the two-person prototype, and it scales the day the firm wants it to. The rule that keeps it honest, measured by the meter below: if a month passes with no FAIL pinned, we've either stopped experimenting or started hiding the misses.
