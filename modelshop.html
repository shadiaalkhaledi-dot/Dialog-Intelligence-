Why one door, three rooms

This used to be one long page called the Playbook -- ground rules, an honest gap analysis, a master plan, a learning plan, and a list of stray ideas, all in six tabs on one scroll. It worked, but it buried three genuinely different jobs under one label: learning from outside precedent, tracking our own state, and holding onto raw ideas that haven't gone anywhere yet.

So: one antechamber, three rooms. Precedents grows every time we research another company. Roadmap is ours -- current state, not aspiration. Ideas is allowed to stay messy, because that's what a pin-up wall of sparks actually looks like before something gets built.

A working record that tells the truth about its own state. Knowledge, not people. Drafted by Shadi & Ben, narrated by Claude. · v2026-07-28.1
